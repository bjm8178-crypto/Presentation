Understanding AI, AI Models, and AI Agents

Objectives

The students should be able to:

A. Define an AI model and a Large Language Model (LLM), identify the types of AI models, and explain how an AI agent differs from a regular AI model;
B. Appreciate the importance of using AI responsibly and how organizations use AI agents to serve people better; and
C. Design a simple AI agent for a school, home, or small-business task using its parts: goal/instructions, AI model, memory, tools, and observing the result and repeating if needed.
